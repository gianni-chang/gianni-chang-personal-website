About

I'm a Business Analyst at the London Borough of Camden, working within the Oracle Cloud Support Team on a wide range of Fusion Cloud workstreams — from Procurement and Purchase-to-Pay through to Analytics, Contracts, and the Redwood transition. My role sits at the crossroads of functional system knowledge, process improvement, and hands-on project delivery.

My background is a little unconventional: I studied Mechanical Engineering before moving into Business Analysis through the Peregrine Novus BA Graduate Scheme, a BCS-aligned programme. That dual foundation — engineering rigour plus structured BA methodology — is exactly how I approach problems: understand the system, map the process, identify constraints, agree the options, deliver a clear plan.

Right now I'm focused on Oracle Fusion delivery across several live workstreams: FAIDP/FDI Analytics implementation, the Redwood UI transition, Responsive Self Service Procurement and Receiving, and user enablement programmes for council staff. There's always something at a different stage of the lifecycle — and I find that variety genuinely energising.

Longer term, I'm building AI fluency — learning to create software, automations, websites, and digital products using AI as a creative partner. My aim is to combine deep domain knowledge in enterprise systems with the speed and flexibility of AI-assisted building, and eventually to create something entirely my own.

Outside work, you'll find me on the field hockey pitch or somewhere along the Mediterranean coast.
